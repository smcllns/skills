The brief

The brief — what was asked, verbatim
best private high schools in the Bay Area for gifted kids who love math, engineering and soccer

No single Bay Area school maxes all three axes, so the ranking trades them off honestly. Menlo is the best all-rounder; Harker wins math/eng + gifted; Bellarmine pairs a soccer dynasty with world-champion robotics but is all-boys; Sacred Heart Prep is elite soccer + solid STEM; Nueva is the only school built for gifted learners; Lick-Wilmerding is the deepest hands-on engineering.

Menlo School

Atherton · co-ed 9–12

The best all-rounder for the brief — the one pick that is genuinely strong on all three axes at once.

  • Math & engineeringApplied Science & Engineering dept (mechatronics, electrical/mechanical eng) in the Whitaker Lab; a fast honors math line into multivariable / linear algebra plus a full CS sequence.
  • SoccerBoys back-to-back WBAL champs & 2025 CCS Div II finalists (12-1-1); girls back-to-back CCS champions (2025–26).
  • Gifted fitHighly selective (~<10% est.), avg SAT ~1440, 100% graduate / 96% to four-year colleges.
  • Tuition~$64,718 (2025–26). Need-based aid offered.
Watch-outMath-competition culture isn't publicly emphasized — a contest-driven mathlete should confirm clubs / AMC directly.

Top pick · West Bay Athletic League · LEED Nichols Hall labs

The Harker School

San Jose · co-ed, Upper School 9–12

The region's STEM and gifted powerhouse; soccer is a real bright spot rather than a dynasty.

  • Math & engineeringNational math standing (USAMO / USAJMO honorees, dozens of AIME qualifiers a year), 10+ advanced CS topics, engineering courses, an Advanced Research program and an annual Research Symposium.
  • SoccerWBAL / CCS. Boys won the program's first-ever CCS title in 2019-20 and reached NorCal; girls took a league title that year. Good, not a perennial power.
  • Gifted fitAmong the most selective in the Bay Area (est. <20%), ~1500 avg SAT, 97–98% AP pass, 52 National Merit semifinalists (2021).
  • Tuition~$64,800 (2025). ~10% of students on aid.
Watch-outFamously high-pressure and pricey; soccer isn't a recruiting pipeline, so serious club players still rely on outside clubs.
Visit Harker ↗ Upper-school STEM ↗
Top pick · West Catholic Athletic League · FRC Team 254

Bellarmine College Prep

San Jose · all-boys, Jesuit 9–12

A rare soccer dynasty paired with a world-champion robotics program — held back for this brief only by being single-gender and CS-light.

  • Math & engineeringHome of FRC Team 254 'The Cheesy Poofs,' a multiple-time FIRST Robotics world champion; 23 AP / 14 Honors, a Maker Lab and an Institute for Innovation & Entrepreneurship.
  • SoccerWCAL / CCS — the most WCAL (29) and CCS (24) soccer titles in the league/section plus 2 NorCal titles; ranked #4 nationally in 2021. A genuine powerhouse.
  • Gifted fitRanked the #1 all-boys school in the Bay Area; rigorous 'Informed Enrollment' honors/AP gating; selective; ~13:1 ratio.
  • Tuition~$33,575 (2026–27) — far below the independents.
Watch-outAll-boys and large (~1,655). High-pressure; deeper standalone CS is limited next to the marquee robotics program.
Visit Bellarmine ↗ Athletics ↗
Top pick · WCAL / WBAL · Homer Science Center

Sacred Heart Prep

Atherton · co-ed, Catholic 9–12

Elite soccer with solid STEM and a co-ed setting; the engineering is strong but more conventional than the maker-heavy schools.

  • Math & engineeringBroad Honors/AP math & science, a Computer Science Integration program plus robotics, anchored by the 44,000-sq-ft Homer Science Center and 'Creative Inquiry' build projects.
  • SoccerBoys back-to-back CCS titles (2021, 2022); girls multiple CCS championships and two USWNT World Cup champion alumnae (Dahlkemper '11, Davidson '16).
  • Gifted fitRigorous Catholic college-prep, seminar-style classes, selective (fully enrolled 2026–27), top-ranked on Niche.
  • Tuition~$59,565 (2026–27, grades 6–12 band).
Watch-outFaith / religious-studies requirement; lighter on hands-on engineering / fabrication than Lick or Nueva.

The Nueva School

San Mateo · co-ed, Upper School 9–12

The one school designed for gifted learners, with standout design-engineering; soccer is its weakest axis.

  • Math & engineeringInnovation Lab (i-Lab) fabrication / makerspace; courses like Design Engineering for Social Good, Machine Learning and an Engineering & Fabrication Workshop; over 85% of the curriculum is electives.
  • SoccerBoys & girls in PSAL (a small-school private league) within CCS — girls won several league titles (2019–25), boys took 2025. Small competitive pool vs WBAL / WCAL.
  • Gifted fitServes 'gifted and talented students since 1967'; 3× National Blue Ribbon; identity built around high-ability learners, not retrofitted services.
  • Tuition~$66,960 (2025–26).
Watch-outAthletics is not the headline (Niche grades it ~C+) — families prioritising elite competitive soccer should weigh this.
Visit Nueva ↗ Innovation Labs ↗
Top pick · Bay Counties League–West · technical arts required

Lick-Wilmerding

San Francisco · co-ed 9–12

The deepest hands-on engineering / technical-arts of any pick — but the weakest soccer of the six.

  • Math & engineeringDescends from the 1874 School of Mechanical Arts; every student does 4+ semesters of technical arts — computing, metal, wood, digital fabrication, electronics — across Design & Engineering tracks.
  • SoccerBCL-West, a small independent-school league. Boys recently 2nd in league with an NCS Div II playoff seed; both boys & girls field varsity. No section titles.
  • Gifted fitHighly selective, test-free admissions (~1,000+ applicants for ~140 seats; est. ~14%), small (~555), top-ranked on Niche.
  • Tuition~$62,250 (2025–26); need-based Flexible Tuition $1k–$50k.
Watch-outSoccer is the weak fit — competitive but low-profile league, no titles. Priciest option.

Appendix B — recommended next steps
  • Visit during the winter soccer season and watch a league match for your top two; confirm boys'/girls' program depth and coaching fit.
  • Ask each school directly about math-competition participation (AMC/AIME, math team) and the engineering/robotics course sequence — depth varies a lot between robotics-led and shop/fab-led programs.
  • For a gifted profile, ask how acceleration and advanced placement actually work in practice (Nueva and the AP-free schools differ markedly).
  • Confirm current-year tuition and financial-aid figures and acceptance criteria directly with admissions — several rates here are third-party estimates.
  • If the child is a girl, re-weight Castilleja into the top tier; if a boy, weigh Bellarmine's all-boys environment deliberately.
